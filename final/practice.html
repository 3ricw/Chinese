How the final works

The guide describes a two-hour, closed-book exam. You hand in Part I before receiving Part II. Part II supplies a lesson-indexed vocabulary list in your chosen script, with characters only. These original practice questions and sample word banks follow the guide’s formats. For the 35% section, also redo your previous lesson tests.
