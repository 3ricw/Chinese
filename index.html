Reading practice

Small shapes, familiar words

一、二、三、四、五。

Yī, èr, sān, sì, wǔ.

One, two, three, four, five.

六、七、八、九、十。

Liù, qī, bā, jiǔ, shí.

Six, seven, eight, nine, ten.

人、口、手、心。

Rén, kǒu, shǒu, xīn.

Person, mouth, hand, heart.

日、月、水、火。

Rì, yuè, shuǐ, huǒ.

Sun, moon, water, fire.

Spot the building blocks

人：你、他。

Rén: nǐ, tā.

Person component: you, he.

女：妈、姐、妹。

Nǚ: mā, jiě, mèi.

Woman component: mother, older sister, younger sister.

口：吃、喝、叫。

Kǒu: chī, hē, jiào.

Mouth component: eat, drink, call.

水：洗、汽。

Shuǐ: xǐ, qì.

Water component 氵: wash, steam.

Reading practice

A first hello

你好！我姓李。

Nǐ hǎo! Wǒ xìng Lǐ.

Hello! My surname is Li.

你叫什么名字？

Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?

What is your name?

我叫王朋。

Wǒ jiào Wáng Péng.

My name is Wang Peng.

你是中国人吗？

Nǐ shì Zhōngguó rén ma?

Are you Chinese?

是，我是中国人。你呢？

Shì, wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén. Nǐ ne?

Yes, I am Chinese. And you?

My teacher

王老师，你好！

Wáng lǎoshī, nǐ hǎo!

Hello, Teacher Wang!

我叫李友。

Wǒ jiào Lǐ Yǒu.

My name is Li You.

我是学生。

Wǒ shì xuésheng.

I am a student.

我也是中国人。

Wǒ yě shì Zhōngguó rén.

I am also Chinese.

Reading practice

Our family photo

这是我家的照片。

Zhè shì wǒ jiā de zhàopiàn.

This is my family's photo.

我家有四个人。

Wǒ jiā yǒu sì ge rén.

There are four people in my family.

这是我爸爸，那是我妈妈。

Zhè shì wǒ bàba, nà shì wǒ māma.

This is my dad; that is my mom.

她是我妹妹。

Tā shì wǒ mèimei.

She is my younger sister.

我爱我家。

Wǒ ài wǒ jiā.

I love my family.

Who is he?

他是谁？

Tā shì shéi?

Who is he?

他是我哥哥。

Tā shì wǒ gēge.

He is my older brother.

他是医生吗？

Tā shì yīshēng ma?

Is he a doctor?

他不是医生，他是律师。

Tā bú shì yīshēng, tā shì lǜshī.

He is not a doctor; he is a lawyer.

我妈妈和爸爸都是老师。

Wǒ māma hé bàba dōu shì lǎoshī.

My mom and dad are both teachers.

Reading practice

Dinner tonight

今天是星期三。

Jīntiān shì xīngqīsān.

Today is Wednesday.

你晚上忙吗？

Nǐ wǎnshang máng ma?

Are you busy tonight?

我不忙。

Wǒ bù máng.

I am not busy.

我们七点半吃饭，怎么样？

Wǒmen qī diǎn bàn chī fàn, zěnmeyàng?

How about eating at seven thirty?

好！晚上见。

Hǎo! Wǎnshang jiàn.

Great! See you tonight.

A birthday

今天是几号？

Jīntiān shì jǐ hào?

What is today's date?

今天是十月九号。

今天是我的生日。

Jīntiān shì wǒ de shēngrì.

Today is my birthday.

Nǐ jīnnián duō dà?

How old are you this year?

我今年十九岁。

Wǒ jīnnián shíjiǔ suì.
